# Skills for IS Research and Development (ISYS90118)

## Course Details

- Semester: 2026 Semester 2
- LMS course: `ISYS90118_2026_SM2`
- Lecturer: Professor Shanton Chang
- Lecture venue: Brown Room, Electrical & Electronic Engineering Building
- Teaching format: Alternating lecture and tutorial weeks

## What Matters

- Conduct sound literature reviews.
- Analyse research and practitioner sources with care.
- Develop research questions and choose sound research methods.
- Write, reflect on, and pitch a research proposal.
- Apply research skills to information systems development, especially requirements analysis and planning.

## Course Focus

The subject introduces lifelong learning and research skills for information systems study and practice. It treats research as a planned process for answering questions and building knowledge. It also shows how research can improve professional practice and information systems development work.

Topics include:

- The nature of research
- Research design
- Information systems development approaches
- Theory and research
- Positivist, interpretivist, and critical research approaches
- Quantitative and qualitative methods
- Experimental research
- Research ethics

## Intended Learning Outcomes

On completing the subject, students should be able to:

- Find peer-reviewed publications and grey literature, then judge their relevance to a research topic.
- Analyse research papers and identify their key messages.
- Bring findings from several publications together to form a research question.
- Research and justify a suitable method for answering a specific research question.
- Judge a research approach against accepted research ethics.
- Reflect on the strengths and weaknesses of a chosen method in a research or development setting.
