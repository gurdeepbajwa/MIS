## Purpose

The journals track how the student's knowledge changes during the semester. Each entry should consider what the student has learned, how that learning occurred, and how it may shape future study or work. An entry may also discuss concerns, gaps in understanding, or steps the student could take in response.

There are no set right or wrong answers. The task calls for personal, deep reflection rather than a standard response or sample answer.

## Optional Areas for Reflection

Students may reflect on:

- Readings completed so far
- Learning from lectures or tutorials
- How the subject may help with future study or work
- Research methods that seem worth exploring
- Surprising or disappointing parts of learning about research
- Their own research process
- Another issue that supports honest reflection on learning

These are prompts, not required sections.

## Submission Rules

- Keep each entry to no more than 250 words.
- Submit a Microsoft Word document or PDF through Turnitin in the LMS.
- References are not required.
- References do not count towards the word limit if used.

## Practical Check Before Submission

- Have I explained what I learned rather than only what the class covered?
- Have I explained how my learning or view changed?
- Have I linked the learning to future work, information systems, research, or my wider study?
- Is the reflection personal and specific?
- Is the entry no more than 250 words?
- Is the file ready as a Word document or PDF for Turnitin?
