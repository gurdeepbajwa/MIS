- Each quiz contains 10 multiple-choice questions.
- Quizzes run in class.
- Students receive a hardcopy bubble sheet and mark answers with a pen.
- The tutor displays one question at a time.
- Students receive a set period to read and answer each question before the tutor moves on.
- Bring a pen to every quiz workshop.
- The official notice does not state the total duration or time allowed per question.
