## Priority Topics

### Week 6

- Write a precise asset–threat–vulnerability–impact scenario.
- Distinguish asset value from scenario impact.
- Explain scope, identification, assessment, control and review.
- Apply a consistent risk matrix and justify likelihood and impact with evidence.
- Distinguish risk appetite and residual risk.
- Recognise the lecture's four treatment labels and their wording differences from earlier tutorials.
- Recall the three practice deficiencies: superficial identification, weak grounding in the real situation, intermittent/non-historical assessment.
- Explain why broad asset labels, informal work and knowledge can escape an assessment.
- Apply these ideas to Horizon Scene 12 and the hospital register.

### Week 7

- Explain policy purpose, authority and its four motivation groups.
- Distinguish policies, standards, procedures and guidelines.
- Recall the Bull's-Eye sequence: policy, networks, systems, applications.
- Distinguish four effectiveness conditions from nine policy-quality attributes.
- Recognise completeness versus accuracy, suitability versus accuracy, and stability versus changeability.
- Explain policy's relationship with culture, risk management, SETA and technical controls.
- Diagnose Horizon Scene 13 using evidence, a business consequence and a named concept; choose a feasible first fix.
