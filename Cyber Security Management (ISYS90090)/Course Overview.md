# Cyber Security Management (ISYS90090)

## Course Details

- Semester: 2026 Semester 2
- LMS course: `ISYS90090_2026_SM2`
- Subject coordinator: Atif Ahmad
- Delivery: Hybrid
- Teaching period: 27 July to 25 October 2026

## What Matters

- Complete each online module and the set textbook reading before the workshop.
- Attend the one-hour weekly lecture.
- Attend the two-hour weekly workshop from Week 02.
- Assessment material will be covered in lectures and workshops, with a strong focus in workshops.

## Weekly Study Pattern

1. Review the week's online module and set reading.
2. Attend the one-hour lecture.
3. Attend the two-hour workshop from Week 02.
4. Optionally join the online peer discussions.

The lecturer has opened the full set of online modules, but may still update the tutorial page for a later week. Recheck each week's module before its workshop rather than relying on an early copy.

## Assessments

| Assessment | Weight | Timing |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Five workshop quizzes | 20% total | Weeks 04, 06, 08, 10, and 11 |
| Group oral presentation | 30% | Week 05, with some presentations possibly in Week 06 |
| Closed-book examination | 50% | Two hours during the exam period |

### Exam Hurdle

Students must pass the exam to pass the subject. A student whose total subject mark reaches 50% but who fails the exam hurdle will receive an `NH` grade and a formal subject mark of 49.

### Exam Practice in Workshops

In the Week 3 lecture, the lecturer said tutors would use parts of the prior year's Section C exam questions for weekly case practice, starting with the Horizon case. The lecturer described Section C as the largest and weakest part of the prior exam. Treat this as guidance on how to prepare, not proof that the 2026 exam will use the same questions or structure.

## Weekly Topics

- Week 01: Modules 0 and 1 — Subject overview and introducing cybersecurity; one-hour lecture.
- Week 02: Module 2 — Why organisations need cybersecurity; one-hour lecture and two-hour workshop.
- Week 03: Module 3 — Understanding the cyber-threat landscape; one-hour lecture and two-hour workshop.
- Week 04: Module 4 — Overview of management practices in organisations; one-hour lecture, two-hour workshop, and quiz.
- Week 05: Module 5 — Role of technological controls; one-hour lecture, two-hour workshop, and group oral presentations.
- Week 06: Module 6 — Risk management in cybersecurity; one-hour lecture, two-hour workshop, and quiz. Some oral presentations may also run this week.
- Week 07: Module 7 — Policy management in cybersecurity; one-hour lecture and two-hour workshop.
- Week 08: Module 8 — Education, training, and awareness in cybersecurity; one-hour lecture, two-hour workshop, and quiz.
- Week 09: Module 9 — Strategy in cybersecurity; one-hour lecture and two-hour workshop.
- Week 10: Module 10 — Responding to cyber incidents; one-hour lecture, two-hour workshop, and quiz.
- Week 11: Module 11 — Advanced topics; one-hour lecture, two-hour workshop, and quiz.
- Week 12: Module 12 — Revision; one-hour lecture and two-hour workshop.
