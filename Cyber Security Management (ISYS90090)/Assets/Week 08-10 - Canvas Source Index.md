## Week 08 Canvas module overview

Canvas page: [C.0 Overview](https://canvas.lms.unimelb.edu.au/courses/237535/pages/c-dot-0-overview?module_item_id=7679749)

The module frames SETA as a managerial control for security behaviour and culture. Its stated outcomes include explaining why SETA helps address threats such as social engineering, distinguishing education, training, and awareness, identifying program steps and implementation challenges, and relating SETA to organisational culture.
