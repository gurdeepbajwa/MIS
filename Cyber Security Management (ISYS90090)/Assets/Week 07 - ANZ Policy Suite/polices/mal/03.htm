Policy

3.1 Software Requirements

3.1.1
All ANZ owned personal computers (PCs) shall run the latest version of the ANZ authorised anti-virus software, configured to ANZ standard.

3.1.2
All privately owned PCs utilised for ANZ business in the home office shall run an ANZ authorised anti-virus software configured to ANZ standard.

3.1.3
All ANZ owned LAN Servers housed on ANZ premises (or offsite locations) shall run the latest version of the ANZ authorised anti-virus software, configured to ANZ standard.

3.1.4
ANZ staff must restart all networked PCs daily.

3.2 Scanning

3.2.1
All content downloaded from non-ANZ sources shall be screened by ANZ authorised anti-virus software prior to being used.

3.2.2
All diskettes and e-mail attachments shall be scanned for viruses before use with ANZ assets.

3.3 Unauthorised Software

3.3.1
ANZ staff shall not load any unauthorised software onto any Standard Operating Environment (SOE) Build machine without prior authorisation.

3.3.2
ANZ staff shall not write, generate, compile, copy, propagate, execute or attempt to introduce any computer software/code designed to self-replicate, damage, or otherwise hinder the performance of any computer's memory, file system or software.

3.4 Escalation Required

3.4.1
ANZ staff shall report all incidents and potential incidents that may come to your attention.

3.4.2
If the incident is malicious software, contact one of the following:

  • TechAssist, or your local Helpdesk
  • GIS - E-mail on "Virus Management & Reporting"
  • Your manager

3.4.3
ANZ staff shall not reply to or forward the suspected malicious software attachment.

3.4.4
ANZ staff shall not open any e-mail suspected or known to contain infected software.

3.4.5
ANZ staff shall not move or copy the suspected malicious software e-mail and/or attachment to other folders.
