Terms

The following terms shall be used:

Term   Definition  
  Breach   Intentional non-compliance with any ANZ Policy and the commission of a criminal offence.  
  Violation   Intentional non-compliance with ANZ policy.  
  Weakness   Potential or suspected breach or violation.  
  Incident   A breach, violation or weakness which poses any risk to ANZ, including financial, reputational, technological or business risks.
