Reporting Incidents

3.1 Process

3.1.1
You shall make a hand written note of any incident as it unfolds.

3.1.2
You shall report all incidents and potential incidents that come to your notice.

3.1.3
If the incident is Malicious software (virus), contact one of the following:

  • TechAssist, or your local Helpdesk
  • GIS - E-mail on "Virus Management & Reporting"
  • Your manager

3.1.4
If the incident is 'account lock-out' related, contact:

  • TechAssist, or your local Helpdesk
  • Notify your manager if the problem persists

3.1.5
If the Incident is a theft of equipment (e.g. laptop) irrespective of the location:

  • Contact the Police and report the theft
  • Notify your manager
  • Contact Group Investigations or Operations Risk Management with details of the Police report.

3.1.6
Regarding the report of any other incident, notify either:

  • Your manager
  • GIS, Executive Manager - E-mail in confidence "GIS - Executive Manager"

3.2 Problem progression

3.2.1
GIS will investigate all incidents and maintain a log of all incidents.

3.2.2
If warranted, GIS will involve other parties such as Human Resources, Group Investigations and Group Legal.
