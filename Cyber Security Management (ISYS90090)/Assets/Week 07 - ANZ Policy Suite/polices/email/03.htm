3. E-MAIL CONTENT AND SECURITY

E-mail Content and Security

3.1 E-mail Usage

3.1.1
E-mail shall only be used for business purposes.

3.1.2
The e-mail system shall not be used for purposes including, but not limited to:
  • Promoting discrimination on the basis of race,
  • gender, colour, national origin, age, marital status,
  • sex, political affiliation, religion, disability or sexual orientation
  • offensive or defamatory content (including pornographic literature)
  • promoting personal, political or religious business or beliefs
  • threatening or violent behaviour
  • personal gain including the conducting of outside business activities
  • harassment or promoting harassment
  • fundraising, or charitable activity not sponsored ANZ
  • destructive activities (e.g. the distribution of malicious software)
  • illegal activities
  • gambling
  • spoofing
  • chain-letters
  • personal announcements
  • spamming (i.e. sending of bulk junk e-mail) and broadcast messages, including general bulk e-mailing, without specific ANZ business purpose

3.1.3
Users of the e-mail system shall not use the mail account of another user to send or receive e-mail.

3.1.4
Users of the e-mail system shall not intercept any other user's e-mail without prior authorisation from GIS.

3.1.5
Information protected by intellectual property law shall not be transmitted via e-mail, unless permission has been expressly and officially provided from the Information Owner.

3.1.6
Signature files attached to e-mail shall not contain any graphics, irrespective of the graphic file type.

3.1.7
Senders of e-mail shall confirm the e-mail address of each recipient and that the recipient is acceptable to the transmission of such information.

3.2 Information Classification

3.2.1
"Highly Restricted" and "Confidential" information shall be encrypted using GIS approved encryption, prior to transmission.

3.2.2
The following classification categories shall be applied to all ANZ information.

Classification   Use  
  Highly Restricted   This classification applies to the most sensitive private information, which, if compromised, poses a major risk, and may cause serious damage and adversely affect ANZ. e.g. Passwords, cryptographic keys, board papers and payments.  
  Confidential   This classification applies to less sensitive private information, which, if compromised, poses a significant risk and/or may adversely affect ANZ. e.g. Customer details, payroll details, and personnel details.  
  Internal   This classification applies to all other private information, which may pose, if compromised, a risk to ANZ. e.g. Internal publications and policies.  
  Public   For information approved for public release by an authorising manager. e.g. Authorised press releases and approved marketing literature.  
       

 

3.2.3
Unless contractual agreements dictate otherwise, messages sent over ANZ computer, and communications systems, are the property of ANZ.

3.3 Information Security

3.3.1
The level of protection for e-mail should be proportionate to the risk to ANZ, taking into consideration the information to be transmitted, the method of transmission and the parties to the exchange.

3.4 E-mail Attachment

3.4.1
E-mail messages must not contain the following types of attachments, including but not limited to .EXE, .COM, .BAT, .DLL, .CMD, .VBS, or .VBE files.

3.4.2
All e-mail attachments shall be scanned for viruses before use with ANZ assets.

3.5 Malicious Software Protection

3.5.1
E-mail shall not be transmitted from or received at workstations that are not equipped and operating with the latest versions of ANZ's virus protection software.

3.5.2
ANZ staff who receive what they believe to be a virus shall e-mail the details to "Virus Management & Reporting".

3.5.3
ANZ staff, who receive an e-mail, which is in contravention to this policy or are aware of misuse of the e-mail system shall contact GIS.

3.6 Sending E-mail

3.6.1
ANZ's e-mail system shall not be used for the transmission of payments or value instructions (including payment authorisations) without the use of approved products for securing and authenticating that transmission. Contact GIS for suitable solutions.

3.6.2
E-mail messages shall not be transmitted in the HTML-Mail/HTML format.

3.6.3
All external e-mail shall contain an ANZ disclaimer statement approved by GIS and Group Legal.

3.7 Generic e-mail Accounts.

3.7.1
Generic e-mail ID's shall not be created.

3.7.2
E-mail products and Internet based e-mail sites, other than those approved for ANZ use, shall not be permitted.

3.8 Outlook

3.8.1
The "out of office assistant" shall only be used within the ANZ internal network.

3.8.2
The "automatic forwarding" rule shall only be used within the ANZ internal network.

3.9 Monitoring

3.9.1
E-mail is monitored by ANZ. ANZ may intercept and review any e-mail composed, sent, received or stored.

3.9.2
ANZ shall examine all data stored in, or transmitted by, these systems within ANZ premises.
