ANZ's Security Principles

We rely on business information for the successful performance of ANZ. We should apply the same high ethics and qualities to the security of our information as we do for all aspects of our work.

Information Security can be likened to a fence. We need to protect against the threats outside of ANZ, including hackers and frauds. We also need to protect what happens to ANZ's information inside our own fence, when it is transferred and when it leaves our care.

Guidance for users on the appropriate action to be taken in the event of an incident concerning ANZ information assets can be found in the Information Security Incident Policy.

Disciplinary action may be taken against individuals where a breach of policy has taken place. Violations may also result in legal action for individuals and ANZ.

Staff are encouraged to notify their manager or Executive Manager, GIS of any concerns regarding information security.
