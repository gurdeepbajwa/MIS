E-mail Structure

3.1 Form Disclaimer

E-mail sent to outside parties such as business letters and certain other "public documents" should include ANZ's full corporate name and the ABN. This information should be in the disclaimer.

The ANZ standard disclaimer statement shall state:

"This e-mail and any attachments to it (the "Communication") is confidential and is for the use only of the intended recipient. The Communication may contain copyright material of Australia and New Zealand Banking Group Limited ABN 11 005 357 522 ("ANZ"), of any of its related entities or of third parties. If you are not the intended recipient of the Communication, please notify the sender immediately by return e-mail, delete the Communication, and do not read, copy, print, retransmit, store or act in reliance on the Communication. Any views expressed in the Communication are those of the individual sender only, unless expressly stated to be those of ANZ. ANZ does not guarantee the integrity of the Communication, or that it is free from errors, viruses or interference."

The ANZ standard disclaimer statement must be attached to all external e-mail, unless a business unit may have specialist requirements that need to be reflected within the disclaimer. Where the ANZ standard disclaimer statement is customised, Corporate Legal and GIS shall approve it prior to its use.

3.2 Stationery Standards

E-mail addresses on business cards and personalised letterhead are to be strictly in terms of the ANZ Graphics Standards.

E-mail addresses on letterhead may only be shown on personalised letterhead for Senior Executive.

3.3 Mailing Lists

Direct subscription to Internet Mail lists are not permitted without authorisation. This includes list digests as well as the more conventional mail lists. Application for subscription to Internet Mail lists may be obtained from the Tech Assist or local Helpdesk, and authorisation from an authorising manager shall be received before approval will be granted.

Only lists with a demonstrated business value may be subscribed to.

3.4 Timeliness

E-mail must not be considered as a guaranteed delivery service. Users should not rely on e-mail for important, urgent or timely messages without checking delivery.

3.5 Mail Volumes

The volume and size of e-mail traffic sent and received through the internal e-mail network and ANZ's Internet Gateway directly affects mail delivery times for the rest of ANZ. For this reason, the following conditions apply:

  • E-mail traffic travelling intersite, within the general CBD area is limited to 2Mb;
  • E-mail traffic travelling between Interstate sites is limited to 2Mb;
  • E-mail traffic travelling through the Internet gateway (inbound and outbound) is limited to 2Mb;
  • E-mail traffic that travels to International sites is limited to 2Mb (some of the slower sites with dial-up connections may be limited to 1Mb).

E-mail sent to an internal employee using their Internet e-mail address, will automatically default to their corresponding internal e-mail address, as it appears in the Global Address List.
