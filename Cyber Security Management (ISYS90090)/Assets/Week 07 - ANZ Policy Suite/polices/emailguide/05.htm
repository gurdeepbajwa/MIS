Roles and Responsibilities

5.1 Policy & Guideline Development

ANZ is the owner of all Policies and GIS is responsible for the continuous development and distribution of all Information Security Policies.

5.2 Management

Each department head, manager and supervisor must ensure that staff are aware of and comply with all policies produced by GIS.

5.3 System Users

It is the responsibility of all ANZ staff to familiarise themselves and comply with GIS Policies. It is a condition of use of the ANZ's systems that users comply with GIS Policies.

If a user of the ANZ system believes something is in contravention of these policies or is otherwise aware of misuse of the system, they shall report the matter to their manager or GIS. This process is outlined in the Information Security Incident Policy.
