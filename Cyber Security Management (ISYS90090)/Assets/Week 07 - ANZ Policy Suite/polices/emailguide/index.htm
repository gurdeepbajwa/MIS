Introduction

1.1 Adherence to Policies

All ANZ staff shall adhere to all relevant policies.

Failure to comply with any authorised policy set by Global Information Security (GIS) may result in an investigation and staff disciplinary action. This is detailed in the 'Information Security Incident Policy', issued by GIS.

All managers are responsible for ensuring that their staff are aware of, and adhere to, these policies.

1.2 Scope

The Information Security Policy is the controlling policy, which shall be adhered to at all times. These guidelines are derived from this and the Electronic Mail policy and aim to assist ANZ staff in understanding these policies.

These guidelines apply to all ANZ staff using any ANZ information assets, including distributed (portable) assets, at the workplace, home or remotely, regardless of the Business Unit, country or system that the information resides in.

These guidelines are relevant irrespective of the form information takes
  • Hardcopy and Printed material
  • Electronic format including Hard disk, Diskette, CDs, Tapes, Back-up media
  • Spoken - voice recording, voice mail and in person
  • In transmission using telecommunications technology e.g. Mobile Phone, SMS Text Messages, WAP, e-mail, and Internet
