Privacy and Confidentiality

While users of ANZ's e-mail system have a general expectation of privacy, the system is not deemed as secure or private. Similarly, even when a message has been deleted from a user's mailbox the message may be recovered from back-up processes.

If there is a requirement to read another user's mail, such as in the case where a user is absent on leave, then a properly authorised person may be given access to the absent colleague's mail. Authorisation should be made by the absent colleague's authorising manager.

4.1 Monitoring

Use of ANZ's e-mail system is monitored. Failure to comply with this or any other authorised policy set by ANZ is viewed seriously and may be cause for an investigation resulting in staff disciplinary action, including termination of employment.
