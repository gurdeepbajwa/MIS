Electronic Security Measures Resources

7.1 Network Security

7.1.1
The clocks of all computers connected to ANZ's internal network shall be synchronised to Universal Co-ordinated Time (UCT) via an ANZ time server, then offset via the computer's operating system, obtaining local standard time of the asset to facilitate auditing of events occurring on that asset.

7.1.2
All diskettes and e-mail attachments shall be scanned for viruses before use with ANZ assets.

7.1.3
ANZ information assets shall run the latest version of the ANZ authorised anti-virus software, configured to ANZ standard.

7.1.4
Dial-up modems shall not be connected to networked assets.

7.1.5
Dial-up privileges shall be approved by both an authorising manager and GIS.

7.1.6
Dial-up modems must be protected with GIS approved user authentication systems.

7.1.7
ANZ staff or authorised third parties establishing an external real-time connection with ANZ internal computers shall authenticate themselves at the ANZ gateway before gaining access to ANZ's internal network.

7.2 Identification and Authentication

7.2.1
Logon IDs shall be unique.

7.2.2
Logon IDs shall be for use by one specific user/system.

7.2.3
New logon IDs and/or passwords shall be transmitted securely to a staff member whose identity has been proven.

7.2.4
Passwords issued from a privileged user shall only be valid for the first time of logging-on.

7.2.5
Passwords shall be kept secret. (As such, all passwords shall have a classification of "Highly Restricted" and handled according to Information Handling Matrix.)

7.2.6
Passwords shall not be displayed in cleartext.

7.2.7
Users shall enter their password manually (i.e. not through the use of macros or 'remember password' scripts).

7.2.8
Only authorised ANZ staff shall use ANZ assets for legitimate business use as stated in the pre-log-in banner.

7.2.9
User-chosen Passwords shall not be reused for at least the next thirteen (13) consecutive changes.

7.2.10
Users shall be allowed three (3) attempts at authentication, before the account is locked-out. Resets shall be gained from a privileged user e.g. TechAssist or your local helpdesk.

7.2.11
Passwords shall be encrypted when held in storage or when transmitted.

7.2.12
Only GIS approved encryption algorithms shall be used.

7.2.13
All vendor supplied passwords, PIN's, Keys, and passphrases shall be changed before being used for ANZ business.

7.3 Access Control

7.3.1
Users shall not invoke operating system level commands without prior executive management authorisation.

7.3.2
All ANZ workstations shall be secured with password protection after fifteen (15) minutes of inactivity.

7.3.3
All ANZ workstations shall be secured with password protection when left unattended.

7.3.4
Diagnostic test hardware and software shall not be used without GIS approval.

7.3.5
Access to and from external resources shall be strictly controlled and monitored.

7.4 Object Re-use

7.4.1
All ANZ information assets, prior to re-use, shall have all residual data erased in accordance with GIS guidelines.

7.5 Disaster Recovery Planning

7.5.1
Managers and Information Owners shall have Disaster Recovery Plans for their information.

7.5.2
Managers and Information Owners shall be responsible for initiating the appropriate Disaster Recovery Plan.
