Information Classification

4.1 Information Classification

4.1.1
The following classification categories shall be applied to all ANZ information.

Classification   Use  
  Highly Restricted   This classification applies to the most sensitive private information, which, if compromised, poses a major risk, and may cause serious damage and adversely affect ANZ. e.g. Passwords, cryptographic keys, board papers and payments.  
  Confidential   This classification applies to less sensitive private information, which, if compromised, poses a significant risk and/or may adversely affect ANZ e.g. Customer details, payroll details, and personnel details.  
  Internal   This classification applies to all other private information, which may pose, if compromised, a risk to ANZ. e.g. Internal publications and policies.  
  Public   For information approved for public release by an authorising manager. e.g. Authorised press releases and approved marketing literature.  
       

It is important to understand that information in all classification categories, with the exception of the "Public" category, is private ANZ information.

4.1.2
Information of various classifications contained within applications and computer systems shall be classified as the highest level classification of information contained therein (e.g. If one file in a system is "Confidential", but the rest are "Internal", information within the system shall be classified as "Confidential".)

4.1.3
Managers or Information Creators shall be responsible for identifying and labelling the classification of the information and applications they use or are responsible for.

4.1.4
ANZ "Highly Restricted" information shall not be disclosed unless expressly authorised by the Information Creator or Executive Management. Other ANZ Information shall not be disclosed without permission from Information Creator or authorising manager.

4.1.5
Information shall be transmitted with regard to its classification. Transmission includes, but is not limited to, transfer through:

  • Electronic exchange
  • Postal service
  • Courier service
  • Internal mail
  • Conversation and telephone
  • E-mail
  • Fax

4.1.6
Information shall be protected and destroyed according to its classification, regardless of storage media. Storage media includes but is not limited to:

  • Hard disk
  • Diskette
  • Printed material
  • Tapes
  • Back-up media
  • CDs
  • Carbon paper and printer ribbons
  • Voice recording and voice mail
  • Microfiche and archived material
