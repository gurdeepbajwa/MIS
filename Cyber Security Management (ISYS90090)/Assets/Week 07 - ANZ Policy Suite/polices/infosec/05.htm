Global Security Environment

5.1 Physical Security

5.1.1
Global Property Services shall be responsible for the provision of physical security protecting ANZ assets.

5.1.2
Physical access to ANZ assets shall be limited to authorised personnel.

5.1.3
ANZ assets shall be protected against human and environmental threats.

5.1.4
All plans, including plans for new buildings, refits, temporary premises shall be referred to GIS for appraisal of the physical information risks.

5.1.5
ANZ information assets must be kept on ANZ property, unless approved by an authorising manager.

5.2 Security Awareness and Training

5.2.1
ANZ managers shall ensure that all ANZ staff are made aware of their security responsibilities.

5.2.2
New ANZ staff shall receive information security training upon commencement of employment.

5.3 Monitoring

5.3.1
ANZ reserve the right to monitor, and record all information traffic moving through the ANZ Global Security Environment.

5.3.2
All records pertaining to security related events shall be reviewed and retained.

5.4 Disaster Recovery Planning

5.4.1
Disaster recovery planning shall exist and be reviewed and tested on a regular basis.

5.5 Legal Requirements and Compliance

5.5.1
ANZ Staff shall notify their manager or GIS of any concerns regarding information.

5.5.2

ANZ staff shall fulfil ANZ's legal obligations as contained within local privacy laws.

5.5.3
All Intellectual Property and rights shall remain with ANZ.

5.5.4
Managers shall ensure that ANZ staff are aware of and adhere to ANZ's legal obligations.
