Our Security Environment

Global Information Security's policies fall into the following categories:

  • Global
  • Local
  • Electronic

3.1 Global Security Policies

3.1.1
These relate to all of ANZ. The Information Security Management Statement (ISMS) signed by the CEO is a high level reflection of global policy. This policy supports the ISMS and further defines global policies, guidelines and standards.

3.2 Local Security Policies

3.2.1
These relate to smaller, demarcated groups of people or places. For example, everyone located at 100 Queen Street or everyone who is an ANZ system user.

3.3 Electronic Security Policies

3.3.1
These relate to the technology we employ. Whether this is your PC, a server or a communication channel.
