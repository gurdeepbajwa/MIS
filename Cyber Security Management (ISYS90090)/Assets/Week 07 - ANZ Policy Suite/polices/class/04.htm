Classification

4.1 Categories

4.1.1
The following classification categories shall be applied to all ANZ information.

 

Classification   Use  
  Highly Restricted   This classification applies to the most sensitive private information, which, if compromised, poses a major risk, and may cause serious damage and adversely affect ANZ. e.g. Passwords, cryptographic keys, board papers and payments.  
  Confidential   This classification applies to less sensitive private information, which, if compromised, poses a significant risk and/or may adversely affect ANZ. e.g. Customer details, payroll details, and personnel details.  
  Internal   This classification applies to all other private information, which may pose, if compromised, a risk to ANZ. e.g. Internal publications and policies.  
  Public   For information approved for public release by an authorising manager. e.g. Authorised press releases and approved marketing literature.  
       

It is important to understand that information in all classification categories, with the exception of the "Public" category, are private ANZ information.

4.2 Reclassification

4.2.1
The Information Creator shall reclassify information, when:

  • Existing classification is inappropriate
  • A change in risk associated with the information occurs
  • A change in the Information classification Policy occurs

4.2.2
The Information Creator shall review the Information classification annually.

4.3 Information Handling

4.3.1
Information shall be labelled according to its classification

  • "Highly Restricted" Information shall display its classification on all pages prior to distribution. Removal of this labelling is prohibited
  • "Confidential" Information shall display its classification on the cover and first pages prior to distribution
  • "Public" documents must be labelled as ANZ Information

4.3.2
Information shall be encrypted according to its classification

  • "Highly Restricted" and "Confidential" Information shall be encrypted whilst on ANZ information assets, at all times, by GIS approved encryption methods

4.3.3
"Highly Restricted" and "Confidential" Information shall not be resident on the Intranet or Internet.

4.3.4
Duplicate copies of "Highly Restricted" Information shall not be made. Copies of other Information shall be approved by Information Creator or Owner.

4.3.5
Original and copied Information shall be destroyed, securely, in accordance with its classification

  • Original "Highly Restricted" Information shall be destroyed by the Information Creator and records kept. The Legal department should verify the retention requirements. The Information Creator shall approve destruction of any copies
  • The Information Creator shall approve the destruction of all copies of "Confidential" Information
  • All other original Information shall be destroyed with the permission of the Information Creator

4.3.6
Hard Copies of Information shall be destroyed according to the classification

  • "Highly Restricted" and "Confidential" Information shall be destroyed by cross shredding or burning
  • "Internal" Information shall be destroyed by shredding or sent for internal secure disposal

4.3.7
Electronic Information shall be destroyed according to its classification

  • "Highly Restricted" and "Confidential" Information shall be destroyed by complete physical destruction or by a magnetic wiping process approved by GIS
