Information

3.1 Creator

3.1.1
The Information Creator is the natural person or other entity that manufactures original information, as an authorised agent of ANZ.

3.1.2
The Information Creator shall be responsible for the following:

  • Classifying and labelling the information
  • Making decisions about the permissible uses of the information including relevant business rules, policies and laws
  • Authorising access to the information based on the need-to-know basis
  • Determining the recovery procedure for the information
  • Ensuring privacy of the information in accordance with its classification

3.1.3
Information Creators may contact GIS for a consultation to determine the appropriate classification of information.

3.2 User

3.2.1
Information Users are individuals who are authorised by ANZ to access, modify, delete and/or utilise information.

3.2.2
The Information Users shall be responsible for the following:

  • The use of information is only for purposes specifically approved by ANZ
  • Ensuring the privacy of information in their possession in accordance with its classification
  • Reporting to the Information Creator, and/or GIS, any information security incidents
