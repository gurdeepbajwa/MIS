4. POLICY

Policy

4.1 Usage

4.1.1 ANZ staff who have been granted Internet access shall not use it for non-business purposes including, games, private business, clubs, schools and other non-business activities within ANZ premises.

4.1.2 ANZ staff shall not use the Internet for purposes including, but not limited to:

  • offensive content including pornographic material
  • promoting discrimination on the basis of race, gender, colour, national origin, age, marital status, sex, political affiliation, religion, disability or sexual orientation
  • threatening or violent behaviour
  • slanderous or defamatory content
  • harassment or promoting harassment
  • destructive activities (e.g. the distribution of malicious software)
  • personal gain including the conducting of outside business activities
  • any other illegal activity
  • promoting political or religious business or beliefs
  • spoofing (misrepresenting, obscuring, suppressing, or replacing a user's identity on the Internet)
  • fundraising, or charitable activity not sponsored by ANZ
  • gambling

4.1.3
ANZ shall log Internet use. ANZ staff visiting a site deemed inappropriate in accordance with this policy shall be subject to disciplinary action.

4.2 Information Confidentiality

4.2.1
ANZ "Internal", "Confidential" and "Highly Restricted" information shall not be sent over the Internet unless it has first been encrypted by GIS approved methods.

4.2.2
ANZ staff using ANZ assets shall adhere to the Telecommuting Security Policy.

4.3 Information Integrity

4.3.1
All Internet content downloaded from non-ANZ sources shall be screened with ANZ approved virus detection software prior to being used.

4.3.2
Automatic updating of software or information on ANZ computers via background "push" Internet technology is prohibited without prior authorisation from GIS.

4.3.3
ANZ staff shall not establish new Internet and Intranet web pages dealing with ANZ business, or make modifications to existing web pages dealing with ANZ business, without prior authorisation from an authorising manager.

4.4 Public Representations

4.4.1
ANZ staff shall utilise the Internet for ANZ Business purposes only.

4.4.2
All publicly posted information must be authorised by the Information Creator.

4.5 Intellectual Property Rights

4.5.1
ANZ staff must presuppose that all materials on the Internet are copyrighted and/or patented unless specific notice states otherwise.

4.5.2
ANZ staff shall not download executable files (software).

4.5.3
ANZ staff shall adhere to software vendors' licence agreements.

4.6 Access Control

4.6.1
ANZ staff connecting to the Internet through PC's connected to the ANZ network (including GRAS or other locally approved method of secure remote access) shall connect through the "ANZ Secure Gateway".

4.6.2
ANZ staff establishing a real-time connection with ANZ internal computers via the Internet shall authenticate themselves using GIS approved systems before gaining access to ANZ's internal network.

4.6.3
ANZ staff shall not employ Internet Service Provider (ISP) accounts and dial-up lines to access the Internet whilst connected to the ANZ network.

4.6.4
ANZ reserves the right to prohibit access to Internet sites and/or pages from ANZ staff's view.

4.6.5
ANZ staff shall not penetrate test security mechanisms at either ANZ or other Internet sites without prior authorisation from GIS.

4.6.6
At any time and without prior notice, ANZ reserves the right to examine web browser cache files, web browser bookmarks, and other information stored on or passing through ANZ computers, within ANZ premises.
