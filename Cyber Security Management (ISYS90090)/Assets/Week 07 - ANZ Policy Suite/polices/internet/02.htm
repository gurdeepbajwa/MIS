Scope

The Information Security Policy is the controlling policy, which shall be adhered to at all times. All other ANZ policies, guidelines and standards shall be derived from the Information Security Policy.

This policy applies to all ANZ staff using any ANZ information assets, including distributed (portable) assets, at the workplace, home or remotely, regardless of the Business Unit, country or system that the information resides in. It covers the responsibilities of all ANZ staff to maintain the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information assets.

This policy is relevant irrespective of the form information takes, including but not limited to:
  • Hardcopy and Printed material
  • Electronic format including Hard disk, Diskette, CDs, Tapes, Back-up media
  • Spoken - voice recording, voice mail and in person
  • In transmission using telecommunications technology e.g. Mobile Phone, SMS Text Messages, WAP, e-mail, and Internet

The Internet is an important resource for ANZ and its employees and although it represents a valuable resource, it also exposes ANZ and ANZ staff to risk. The protection of proprietary information, the isolation and security of internal systems and productivity are of the utmost importance.

ANZ may be held accountable for abusive, inappropriate or unethical behaviour of ANZ staff accessing the Internet from, or using, ANZ's facilities. This policy defines responsibilities, access controls, acceptable use and conduct once ANZ staff are connected to the ANZ network or using any ANZ equipment. Its purpose is to clearly communicate ANZ's policy regarding what is and what is not "acceptable use".
