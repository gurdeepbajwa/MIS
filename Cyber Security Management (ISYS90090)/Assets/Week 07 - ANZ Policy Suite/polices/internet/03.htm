Management Approval

All Internet access shall be authorised by the user's authorising manager. Approval shall be granted on the following basis:

3.1 Business Usage

  • Business use is any activity necessary to meet ANZ business objectives
  • Business research
  • Specific data retrieval from business related sites
  • Membership of a closed or open discussion forum/groups in which information or ideas are shared between organisations relating to business

3.2 Technical Business Usage

  • Technical research relating to specific product, policy or technical direction
  • Technical data retrieval
