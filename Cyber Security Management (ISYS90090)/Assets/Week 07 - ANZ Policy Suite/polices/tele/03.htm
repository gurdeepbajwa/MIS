Requirements

(for further information email "Access Control Services")

3.1 Authorisation

3.1.1
Only ANZ staff approved by authorising managers shall telecommute.

3.1.2
Access to ANZ networks shall only be gained through GRAS dial-up facility or your locally approved method of secure remote access.

3.1.3
Telecommuters shall comply with all ANZ operational policies, standards and procedures.

3.1.4
ANZ staff establishing a real-time connection with ANZ internal computers via GRAS dial-up facility or your locally approved method of secure remote access, shall authenticate themselves at the secure gateway before gaining access to ANZ's internal network.

3.2 Hardware

3.2.1
ANZ owned hardware shall conform to ANZ's minimum remote (home) PC requirements.

3.2.2
Privately owned hardware shall conform to ANZ's minimum remote (home) PC requirements.

3.2.3
ANZ hardware shall not be altered or added to in any way unless approved by Tech Assist or your local helpdesk.

3.2.4
Incident logs shall be maintained to record equipment or software faults.

3.2.5
ANZ staff shall make available all relevant hardware for inspection as requested.

3.3 Software

3.3.1
All Software shall conform to ANZ's minimum remote (home) PC requirements.

3.3.2
ANZ staff shall not change the operating system configuration or install new software on ANZ owned hardware without approval from Tech Assist or your local helpdesk.

3.3.3
ANZ staff shall not download software from the Internet, or other systems outside ANZ onto ANZ owned hardware without approval from Tech Assist or your local helpdesk.

3.3.4
No addition, modification or deletion to software is permitted unless approved by Business Unit management.

3.3.5
ANZ staff shall not use ANZ assets for private purposes or for the playing of games.

3.3.6
Whenever employment with ANZ is terminated (voluntarily or otherwise), all ANZ assets shall be returned on (or before, if so instructed) the last day of active employment.
