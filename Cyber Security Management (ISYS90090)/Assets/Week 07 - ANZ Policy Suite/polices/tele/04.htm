4. SECURITY

Security

4.1 Physical Environment Conditions

4.1.1
ANZ information shall be protected in a manner commensurate with its sensitivity, value, and critically, irrespective of the storage media on which information is recorded, the locations where the information is stored, the systems used to process the information, or the processes by which the information is handled.

4.1.2
ANZ's physical and information assets shall not be visible to others from inside or outside the work location.

4.1.3
Reasonable precautions shall be taken to protect ANZ assets from theft, damage, and misuse.

4.1.4
While in the home office and not in use, equipment and information shall be kept under lock and key.

4.1.5
ANZ staff shall not leave workstations unattended when an active logon session is in progress on the ANZ network via GRAS (or your locally approved method of secure remote access).

4.1.6
Unattended workstations shall be logged off (if attached to a network) and turned off.

4.1.7
ANZ staff shall make regular backups of data which must be stored in a secure and environmentally safe location.

4.1.8
ANZ staff telecommuting shall report any damage of ANZ assets or loss of information to their manager, or GIS immediately.

4.1.9
ANZ staff telecommuting shall report any loss of ANZ assets to the Police and their manager, Group Investigations or Operation Risk immediately.

4.1.10
All "Confidential" information must be disposed of in a secure manner.

4.2 Portable Computers

4.2.1
ANZ staff shall not leave portable computers containing "Highly Restricted" and "Confidential" information unattended at any time unless the information is stored in encrypted form.

4.2.2
ANZ staff shall avoid working on ANZ "Highly Restricted" and "Confidential" information in public and unsecured locations.

4.3 Information Security

4.3.1
Suitable physical protection shall be afforded to ANZ's information assets.

4.3.2
ANZ Information shall not be removed from ANZ locations without the Information Creator's approval regardless of the form it takes i.e. hard disk, floppy disk, hard copy or memos.

4.3.3
Information classified "Highly Restricted" or "Confidential" that is stored on a local drive shall be protected against unauthorised access; it shall be encrypted.

4.3.4
Information regarding access to the ANZ's computer and communication systems, such as dial-up modem phone numbers must remain "Highly Restricted".

4.3.5
Information regarding access to the ANZ's computer and communication systems, such as dial-up modem phone numbers shall be changed immediately if compromised.
