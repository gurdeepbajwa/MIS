  • staff refers to full time, part time, casual and temporary employees, contractors, sub contractors and authorised representatives of ANZ.

1.5 Adherence to this Policy

This policy has been authorised as the definative Telecommuting Security Policy by Global Information Security (GIS).

All ANZ staff shall adhere to the Telecommuting Security Policy.

Failure to comply with this or any other authorised policy set by GIS may result in an investigation and staff disciplinary action. This is detailed in the 'Information Security Incident Policy', issued by GIS. All managers are responsible for ensuring that their staff are aware of and adhere to this policy.

GIS is responsible for setting and interpreting policies on information security throughout ANZ. All queries relating to this policy shall be made through GIS. Copies of authorised policies can be obtained by contacting GIS.

1.6 Deviation

Where compliance to policy is not possible in order to meet ANZ business objectives or a specific issue may not be addressed within this policy, a request to deviate may be submitted to "GIS-Business Security Management".
