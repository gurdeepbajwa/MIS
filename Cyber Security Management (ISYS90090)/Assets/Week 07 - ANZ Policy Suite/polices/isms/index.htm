Information Security Management System

 
 

We rely on computer systems and the information they contain. The information we hold and collect will advance our strategy, as effective market leaders. We need to protect this information from disclosure, corruption and loss.

Information Security Management can be likened to a security fence around our information. However, the fence is only as strong as its weakest link.

Therefore, in order to sustain this fence and the corresponding links, a group called Global Information Security (GIS) was formed. Their primary objective is to ensure that we are all fully aware of our responsibilities with regards to maintaining this fence and the links that encompass it. Further, it is imperative that we understand the importance of individual accountability in order for us to effectively maintain the strength of this fence.

The purpose of our policies is to protect all information assets from the threats they are exposed to - irrespective of whether the threats are inside or outside our protective fence, or whether they are deliberate or accidental.

The policies

It is ANZ's policy that:

  • All Information Security Incidents will be reported to and investigated by the GIS Executive Manager;
  • Regulatory and legislative requirements will be met;
  • Information assets will be protected against unauthorised disclosure;
  • The integrity of information assets will be maintained;
  • Business requirements for the availability of information will be met;
  • Business Continuity Management Plans will be adhered to;
  • All managers are directly responsible for implementing the policy within their business areas, and for the adherence by their staff;
  • All staff are to adhere to these policies.

The GIS Executive Manager is responsible for maintaining all information security policies, and for providing advice on its implementation. Further policies support this Information Security Management Statement.

Further details of your Information Security responsibilities can be gained from your Manager, from the Intranet or sought from GIS.
